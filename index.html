KilnBook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

KilnBook is a glaze recipe and firing notebook for ceramic artists. This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: your studio data stays on your device. We do not run servers, we do not have accounts, and we cannot see anything you record.

Data we collect

None. The developer of KilnBook collects no personal data, no usage analytics, no crash telemetry, no advertising identifiers, and no location data. There is no sign-up and no user account.

Data you create, and where it lives

Everything you enter — glaze recipes, materials and percentages, firing programs, firing logs, test tiles, pieces, inventory, notes and photos — is stored locally on your device only, inside the app's private storage. It is never uploaded anywhere.

  • Photos and camera. The app asks for camera and photo access only when you choose to attach a photo to a test tile or a piece, or when you scan a KilnBook QR label. Images are resized and saved into the app's local database. They are not transmitted off the device.
  • Export. If you use "Export data", the app writes a JSON file to your device's Documents folder (on iPhone: the Files app, On My iPhone > KilnBook). Sharing that file afterwards is entirely your choice and under your control.
  • Share recipe card. This generates an image on your device and hands it to the system share sheet. Where it goes next is determined by the app you pick.
  • Deleting data. Uninstalling KilnBook permanently deletes all of its local data. Because there is no cloud copy, we cannot recover it for you — please use Export to keep your own backups.

Purchases

KilnBook Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by the store you installed the app from: Google Play Billing on Android and Apple In-App Purchase on iPhone. The app never sees or stores your payment details. To determine whether you own Pro, the app asks the store what your Google account or Apple ID has purchased. That exchange is between your device and Google or Apple; the developer receives no personal data from it. The transaction is governed by the Google Privacy Policy or the Apple Privacy Policy respectively.

Network access

KilnBook works fully offline. It only goes online when the store's billing service verifies or restores a purchase. No other network requests are made by the app.

Third parties

There are no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or social login providers in KilnBook, and the app does not track you across apps or websites. The only third-party services involved are the store billing services described above.

Children

KilnBook is a tool for ceramic studio work and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example if optional cloud sync is added in a future version — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change. Any feature that would send data off your device would be opt-in.
