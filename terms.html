What the app does

KilnBook is a notebook for ceramic studio work. It stores glaze recipes, firing programs, firing logs, test tiles, pieces and material inventory on your device, scales recipes to batch weights, draws firing curves, and estimates glaze chemistry and firing costs.

Estimates and safety

Batch weights, water amounts, chemistry figures (UMF, oxide ratios, the Stull chart position), cone temperatures, firing durations and costs are calculated from the numbers you enter and from typical material analyses. Real materials, kilns and pyrometers vary. The figures are a guide, not a guarantee: always test before relying on a result, follow your kiln manufacturer's instructions, and handle ceramic raw materials with appropriate protection.
